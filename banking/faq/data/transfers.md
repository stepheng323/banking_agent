# Category: Transfers

## How do bank transfers work?

When you ask {app_name} to send money, it resolves the recipient, asks you to review the amount and account details, and requires the needed confirmation before debiting your linked account. The money is then sent through the available payment provider to the recipient's bank account.

## What are the transfer limits?

Transfer limits depend on the active capability policy, your account, the provider, and any bank restrictions. {app_name} checks applicable limits during the transfer flow and blocks or corrects requests that are outside the allowed range.

## Are there any transfer fees?

Fees can depend on the provider, bank, amount, and current policy. When a fee is available before processing, {app_name} shows it before you confirm the transfer.

## How long do transfers take?

Most transfers complete within seconds or a few minutes. Some bank confirmations can take longer. If a transfer remains pending or fails, ask {app_name} to check the transaction status or create a support ticket.

## Can I cancel a transfer?

You can cancel before final confirmation. Once a transfer has been confirmed and submitted to the provider, it usually cannot be cancelled from chat.

## What is a transfer reference?

A transfer reference is a unique identifier for a transaction. It helps support find the exact transfer if you ask about a failure, pending status, receipt, refund, or dispute.

## Can I transfer to any bank in Nigeria?

{app_name} supports transfers to banks and institutions available through the configured provider. Availability can change, so {app_name} verifies bank and account details during the transfer flow.

## What happens if I enter wrong account details?

{app_name} checks the recipient account name before asking you to confirm. Review the name, bank, and amount carefully. Once you confirm a transfer to the wrong account, recovery may require the receiving bank or recipient.

## How do I know my transfer was successful?

{app_name} shows the transfer status in chat. You can also ask for the status of a recent transaction, request transaction details, or ask for a receipt for an eligible successful transfer.

## What is a narration?

A narration is an optional note attached to a transfer. When supported by the provider and receiving bank, it helps the recipient understand why the money was sent.
