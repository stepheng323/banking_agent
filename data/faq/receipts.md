# Category: Receipts

## How do I get a receipt for my transfer?

After an eligible successful transfer, {app_name} can send a digital receipt in chat. You can also ask for a receipt later by mentioning the transfer or asking for the receipt for your last successful transfer.

## What information is on my receipt?

A receipt may include:

- Transaction reference
- Date and time
- Amount transferred
- Sender details
- Recipient name, bank, and account details
- Transfer status
- Narration, when provided

## Can I get a receipt for past transactions?

Yes, if the transaction is an eligible successful transfer created through {app_name}. Ask for the receipt and include the transaction reference or enough details to identify it.

## Are receipts valid for official purposes?

{app_name} receipts are generated from transaction records and can be used as proof of payment in many situations. For formal documentation, you may still need a bank statement from your bank.

## Why didn't I receive my receipt?

Receipts are available only after eligible successful transfers. If you did not receive one, ask {app_name} to check the transaction status, identify the eligible transfer, or send the receipt again if the transfer qualifies.

## Can I download my receipt as a PDF?

PDF receipt export is not available in chat right now. You can save the receipt image or ask {app_name} to show the transaction details.

## How long are receipts stored?

{app_name} keeps transaction records needed to show transaction details and eligible receipts. Availability can depend on retention policy and the type of transaction.
