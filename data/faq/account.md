# Category: Account

## How do I link my bank account?

To link your bank account:

1. Start the account linking process in the app
2. Select your bank
3. Authorize a direct debit mandate through your bank's secure portal
4. Once approved, your account is ready to use

## Which banks can I link?

Most Nigerian commercial banks are supported, including:

- First Bank, GTBank, Zenith Bank, Access Bank, UBA
- And many more

During linking, you'll see the full list of available banks.

## What is a direct debit mandate?

A direct debit mandate is an authorization you give to allow us to debit funds from your account when you initiate transfers. It's secured by your bank and requires your approval.

## Can I link multiple accounts?

Yes, you can link multiple bank accounts. This allows you to:

- Choose which account to transfer from
- Split large transfers across accounts
- Have backup payment options

## How do I set a default account?

You can set any linked account as your default. The default account is used automatically for transfers unless you specify otherwise.

## How do I unlink an account?

To unlink an account, contact support. We'll guide you through the process and revoke the direct debit mandate.

## Why was my account linking rejected?

Account linking may be rejected if:

- Your bank doesn't support direct debit
- There was a technical issue with authorization
- Your bank account has restrictions

Try again or contact support for help.

## Is there a minimum balance required?

Your linked account needs sufficient funds plus any bank charges to complete transfers. We check available balance before processing.

## How do I check my linked accounts?

Ask me "show my accounts" or "which accounts are linked" to see all your linked accounts.
